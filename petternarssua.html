Ministry & Background

Sir Knight Petter Nars Sua (Sem. Petter Nars Sua) is an active, dedicated altar server with the Santa Barbara Altar Knights (SBAK) ministry at Santa Barbara Parish Church. He belongs to Batch 2025 of SBAK, is a seminarian of the Congregatio Filiorum Sanctae Mariae Immaculatae (FSMI), and serves actively with Youth for Christ (YFC).

Motivational Message to the Youth

Petter frequently serves as an inspiration and mentor for incoming altar servers and YFC members, sharing this message:

"To the young servers, do not be discouraged by waiting or challenges. Serve with sincerity and love. What you learn at the altar today will guide you in becoming a better person tomorrow."
